# Intended game: a day with an animal friend
Audience: a non-reading child around 3 years 4 months, with stronger Hindi, familiar animal vocabulary, a parent present, and a practical need for English communication. The game is calm, offline, touch-led and optional-speech. It is not an assessment.

## Three visible levels
1. Meet the animals: original names and picture recognition.
2. Animal actions: original illustrated cow/dog/rabbit eating, walking and sleeping. Preserve grass/carrot/food poses, movement, replay and recorded language.
3. A day with my animal: choose Rabbit or Cow, then follow that same character through familiar routines. The hero is the continuity; home/school are settings. All levels remain equally discoverable and freely revisitable. Additional sentence/alphabet activities remain available.

## Story destination
A recognisable day: waking and greeting family → getting ready (brushing, making the bed, washing/dressing) → breakfast → travel to school → greet teacher/friends, request help/water, take turns → return home and tidy/play → bedtime. This is a long-term content direction, NOT a requirement to complete the whole day in one session. CURRENT_STATE.md identifies shipped beats.

The existing attractive lakeside classroom is preserved as the school chapter. Reuse existing character sheets, action animations, audio and school missions before producing replacements. Different animals get small meaningful variations within the same storyline. No forced level unlock, graduation based on taps, or place-based curriculum replaces this structure.

## Interaction
An illustrated situation, one spoken cue in the chosen instruction language, one or two large picture options, a meaningful action animation, a short English model and quiet time. Explore offers a gentle example; Listen omits it. Hindi meaning is explicit and separate. A child can point, gesture, speak Hindi/English or simply watch. The game never waits for recognised speech.

Each outcome has a small parent bridge to the child's day. Pretend animals do human routines; do not teach these as biological facts. Habits need real-world practice with a grown-up. Adult support, comfort and connection take precedence over completing a routine.

## Session and progress
Six completed turns or the selected 3/5/7 minute wall-clock limit across every level. The timer includes time away. Same-day stop remains; next local day starts a new visit. Parents can restart immediately for testing. Story checkpoints, chosen character, settings and observations survive restart/day change/update. Chapter replay is available; successful taps are not proof of language mastery.

No account, runtime AI service, microphone, ads, background music, streaks or competitive rewards. After initial installation/download, the game runs offline with no computer server.
